Light the Beacons — player privacy information
Version 1.5.0 / 2026-11-10

What leaves your client
All addon transmissions use WoW's GUILD addon-data channel (prefix LTB1).
There is no external telemetry, analytics endpoint, web request, or ordinary
chat posting in the addon. The game supplies your character name to recipients.

Background location sharing is ON BY DEFAULT. About every 10 seconds while
you are in a guild, the addon sends a timestamp, map ID and in-game coordinates,
even when you have not lit a beacon. These are game positions, not GPS or your
real-world location. Turn off "Share my location with guildmates" under
/ltb locations (Options > Map & beacon controls) to stop background sharing.

Lighting a beacon shares its ID, timestamp, incident map/coordinates, reason
(aid, fallen, or PvP), and any optional message you send. Answering shares your
response and the beacon being answered with the guild, not just the caller.
Cancellation and location-withdrawal packets also go to the guild. Automatic
PvP beacons are off by default; enabling them can send an incident location
after a qualifying death. Saved message templates are not attached automatically.

Audience and limits
"Private guild" means guild-channel scope, not end-to-end encryption or a
per-recipient access list. A guildmate with another addon that listens for this
prefix can receive and record these packets. Don't put sensitive personal
information in a beacon message. Hiding/silencing a player or hiding map markers
only changes your display; it does not prevent that guildmate receiving your
broadcasts. Friend marks and Friends-only filtering are local display choices;
they do not make your location visible exclusively to Friends. Global beacon
mute suppresses local notifications, not outgoing beacons or background sharing.
Your discovered flights, Friend marks, filters, saved templates and travel timings
are not transmitted. Templates are sent only when used in a beacon.

Stopping sharing
Turning background sharing off sends a withdrawal; existing markers expire
within 45 seconds in unmodified Light the Beacons clients if delivery is delayed.
A lit beacon still shares its incident location until extinguished or expired
(five minutes). For no position broadcasts, turn off background sharing and
automatic PvP alerts, extinguish your active beacon, and do not light new calls.
This cannot erase information already received or recorded by another player.

What is stored locally
WoW saves LightTheBeaconsDB per character in its WTF/SavedVariables files.
It holds settings, discovered flight points, learned flight connections/times,
three optional message templates, Friend marks/player filters with character names, button
position, a guild identifier, cooldown/response bookkeeping, and your own active
beacon including its message and responder names. Expired or invalid saved
beacons are discarded at login; unexpired ones are restored so you can cancel
them. Live expiry and cancellation also clear your own beacon record. WoW
writes these changes to disk on its normal save/reload/logout cycle.

Other players' beacons and background locations are kept in session memory,
not saved as location history. Location records expire after 45 seconds and
beacons after five minutes. Changing/leaving guild clears active guild data.
Templates, filters and settings remain until changed or reset. Local backups
and WoW's saved-variable backup files may retain older data.

Removing saved data
Disable the addon and close WoW before removing this character's
LightTheBeacons.lua and LightTheBeacons.lua.bak SavedVariables files if you
want a complete reset. This also removes settings, templates and learned flight
data. Do not delete your whole WTF folder. Other players' copies are unaffected.

Scope
This describes the addon, not Blizzard's, GitHub's or CurseForge's own data
practices. It is based on source review and simulated-client checks; live guild
delivery and other players' software cannot be guaranteed by these checks.
